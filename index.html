A NOTE ABOUT THE MAKER

Curious by
nature. Practical
by choice.

MADE WITH
INTENT✳

I’m Tahsin — an independent developer from Chattogram, Bangladesh, interested in the space between a good idea and a product people can actually use.

I move between interface design, frontend development and the practical systems behind a product: data, admin workflows, integrations and the small decisions that make something feel finished.

I like clear thinking, expressive visual systems and tools that solve a real problem without making the user work harder than necessary.
