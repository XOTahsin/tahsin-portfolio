01 / THE CONTEXT

Make the study workflow easier to manage.

Study tasks often live in separate places: a routine in one note, syllabus progress somewhere else, revision cards in another tool and focus sessions on a timer. StudyPro brings these pieces together around one learning workflow.

PRODUCT INTENT — Reduce the friction between planning a study session and actually getting started.
02 / THE APPROACH

One workspace, connected study tools.

The experience is organized around the actions a student repeats: plan, track, revise and focus. Each tool has a clear purpose while contributing to one personal learning space.

  • Bring routines and syllabus progress into a single view.
  • Use flashcards as a lightweight revision companion.
  • Keep focused work sessions close to the study plan.
03 / PRODUCT DETAILS

Designed around the real workflow.

The product concept combines practical study utilities rather than relying on a single feature. The emphasis is on clarity, quick access and a workflow that can be revisited every day.

  • Study routine planning and tracking.
  • Syllabus progress organization.
  • Flashcard bank for revision.
  • Pomodoro-style focus sessions.
  • User authentication connected to Supabase.
04 / TECHNOLOGY

The tools behind it.

The project uses a web-first approach with JavaScript and a Supabase-backed authentication layer. The live deployment is hosted on Vercel.
